Moving Checklist

Keep this list handy to avoid last-minute surprises.

  1. Set your budget and target rent range.
  2. Pick 2-3 neighborhoods based on commute and lifestyle.
  3. Tour apartments virtually or in person.
  4. Prepare documents: ID, proof of income, references.
  5. Apply for housing and set a move-in date.
  6. Book movers or reserve a truck 2-3 weeks ahead.
  7. Transfer utilities and internet before move day.
  8. Update address for bank, employer, and subscriptions.
  9. Pack essentials bag for your first 48 hours.
  10. Do a final walkthrough and document apartment condition.
